# Agent Receipt — 60-second commercial

Format: screen recording of the real app with a voiceover (you, or an AI voice
reading this). On-screen text is short; the voice carries it. Runs ~60 s at a
calm pace. Square or vertical crop works: every shot is one card of the app.

---

**[0:00 – 0:06] Hook**
*Screen:* black, then the words appear one by one.
*Text on screen:* You let an AI do things for you today. Do you know what it did?
*Voice:* You let an AI do things for you today. Do you know what it did?

**[0:06 – 0:14] The problem**
*Screen:* a quick montage — an AI coding tool editing files, a scheduled task running at 3:12 AM, a card statement line "$49.00".
*Text:* It wrote files. It ran commands. It spent money. Nobody was watching.
*Voice:* It wrote files, ran commands, sent emails, maybe spent money — some of it while you were asleep. And there's no receipt.

**[0:14 – 0:22] The product**
*Screen:* the Agent Receipt statement for one day — the one-line rows, newest first.
*Text:* Agent Receipt. A bank statement for what your AI did.
*Voice:* Agent Receipt is a bank statement for your AI. Every action, one line: what happened, when, which agent — and whether a person was there or not.

**[0:22 – 0:30] What it hooks up to**
*Screen:* the Settings page — the "Agents on this Mac" card showing Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Cowork as *found*; then the connector cards.
*Text:* Claude · Cursor · Codex · Cowork — found automatically. Then: its mailbox, its card, its calendar, its GitHub.
*Voice:* It finds the AI tools on your Mac by itself — nothing to set up. Then you give each agent its own mailbox, its own card, its own calendar, its own GitHub account — and whatever those do lands on the receipt with the agent's name on it.

**[0:30 – 0:38] Needs review**
*Screen:* the Needs review page — a flagged purchase, a "nobody at the keyboard" action; then a macOS notification pops.
*Text:* Flags what deserves a look. Tells you within minutes.
*Voice:* When something can't be undone and nobody was at the keyboard, or an agent spends over the limit you set, it's flagged — and your Mac tells you within five minutes.

**[0:38 – 0:46] Control**
*Screen:* the Agents page — one agent gets **Give a card** ($25 per day); cut to the Stop page — **Suspend the agent's card**, then **Retire**.
*Text:* A budget per agent. One click to stop it. Nothing is ever erased.
*Voice:* Give an agent a budget and its own card. If it misbehaves, suspend the card or retire the agent with one click. The record stays — a receipt that forgets isn't a receipt.

**[0:46 – 0:54] Why it matters**
*Screen:* the Money page, then "Download for QuickBooks / Xero"; then the CSV with the checksum line; then a printed statement.
*Text:* For your bookkeeper. For your insurer. For your clients.
*Voice:* Hand it to your bookkeeper. Show it to your insurer. Send it to a client who asks "what exactly did the AI do with my account?" Every line is checkable, and the logs are checked for tampering.

**[0:54 – 1:00] Close**
*Screen:* the front page, then the GitHub page with the Sponsor button.
*Text:* Free. Open source. Runs on your Mac — nothing leaves it.
*Voice:* Free, open source, runs on your Mac and nothing leaves it. Agent Receipt. Know what your AI did.

## Recording notes
- Use the demo dataset (ask for it) so every screen tells the story without your real project names.
- Dark mode reads better on phones; keep the browser at ~1100 px wide so the one-line rows stay one line.
- Each shot is one page of the app — no scrolling mid-shot except the statement list.
- Say the numbers slowly; "within five minutes" and "nothing leaves it" are the two lines people remember.
