Who has acted on this Mac

Identities come from the agents' own logs, from credentials the receipt reads (a mailbox, a card, a Google account), or from what an agent declares through the inbox — they are observed, not issued. Give an agent a name of your own ("Bookkeeping bot") and that name is used on every statement, alert and export; the log's own label stays underneath. {% if ramp_mode %}Give a card hands an agent its own Ramp fund — a budget that issues a virtual card — so its purchases are its own by construction and can be cut off on their own ({{ ramp_mode }}; docs/RAMP.md).{% endif %} Retire says "from now on this agent is not supposed to act": nothing it did is removed or hidden, its rows stay on every statement, and anything it does afterwards is flagged for review. Retiring does not stop the agent — that takes revoking its mailbox, card or account at the issuer.
