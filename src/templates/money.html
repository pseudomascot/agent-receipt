For your bookkeeper. These amounts are already on your bank, card or Stripe statement — that stays the source of truth. What this page adds is who spent or received the money, for which project, and whether anyone was at the keyboard. The QuickBooks / Xero file is the plain three-column layout (Date, Description, Amount; money out is negative) their bank-import screen accepts; import it and match each line to the bank transaction rather than adding it, so nothing is counted twice. Spent = purchases the receipt saw (card alerts, the agent's own card). Received = payments that came in (Stripe charges).
